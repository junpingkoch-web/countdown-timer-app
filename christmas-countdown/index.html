Where does Christmas come from?

Christmas is celebrated on December 25th in most of the world, marking the Christian commemoration of the birth of Jesus. The date sits close to the winter solstice, and many of its familiar customs — evergreen trees, lights, feasting, and gift-giving — blend that religious origin with much older midwinter folk traditions found across Europe.

Today it's observed on very different scales depending on where you are: a public holiday with closed shops and travel rushes in many countries, a quieter family occasion elsewhere, or simply a cultural and commercial season regardless of personal belief. That's part of why a shared countdown is useful — the date is fixed, even when the way people mark it isn't.

How this countdown works

The countdown targets midnight on December 25th in your own device's time zone. It's calculated live — nothing is fetched from a server — so once Christmas Day has passed, reloading this page will automatically start counting down to next year's Christmas instead.
