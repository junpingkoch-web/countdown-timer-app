Tips & how to use

Timer

  • Set minutes and seconds, then press Start to begin the countdown.
  • Use Pause / Resume to stop and continue without losing the remaining time.
  • Reset at any time to go back to your chosen duration.

Date Countdown

  • Add as many events as you like — each one keeps counting live in the background.
  • Events are sorted automatically, soonest first.
  • Click "Delete" on a card to remove an event you no longer need.

Good to know

  • Your countdowns and language choice are saved automatically in this browser, so they're still here next time you visit.
  • Use the language button in the top right to switch the whole page between English and German.
