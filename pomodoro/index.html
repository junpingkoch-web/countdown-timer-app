What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management method built around short, focused work sprints. You work for a fixed stretch — traditionally 25 minutes — then take a short break, typically 5 minutes, before starting the next round. After four rounds, you take a longer break.

The idea is simple: a visible, ticking countdown makes a work session feel like a short sprint instead of an open-ended slog, which makes it easier to start and easier to resist checking your phone until the timer runs out.
