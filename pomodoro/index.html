What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management method built around short, focused work sprints. You work for a fixed stretch — traditionally 25 minutes — then take a short break, typically 5 minutes, before starting the next round. After four rounds, you take a longer break.

The idea is simple: a visible, ticking countdown makes a work session feel like a short sprint instead of an open-ended slog, which makes it easier to start and easier to resist checking your phone until the timer runs out.

How to use this timer

Leave it on the default 25:00, or set your own minutes and seconds, then press Start. Use Pause if you're interrupted — it won't lose your remaining time — and Reset whenever you want to go back to your chosen duration.

Everything runs locally in your browser: there's no account, no installation, and nothing is sent to a server. The only thing to keep in mind is that the countdown doesn't save its progress if you reload the page, and it only shows an on-screen banner when time is up — no sound, no system notification.
